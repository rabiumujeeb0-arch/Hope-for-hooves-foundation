About us

Our mission and values

Hope for Hooves Foundation rescues injured, neglected and abandoned horses and gives each one the veterinary care, rehabilitation and time it needs to recover. We believe every horse deserves a second chance, however serious its condition when it arrives.

Care first

Every decision starts with the horse's welfare and the advice of our veterinary team.

Honest accounts

We publish how donations are used and how each horse is progressing.

Patient recovery

Rehabilitation takes months. We commit to every horse until it is ready for its next home.
